# This mod adds L4D-styled muzzleflashes to illuminate the area in front of the player when shooting, this is fully clientside and will not appear on or for other players.
## NOTE: USING HIGH ROF WEAPONS MIGHT CAUSE THE EFFECT TO STAY BECAUSE THE CODE/GAME OR BOTH MAY NOT BE ABLE TO PROCESS QUICKLY ENOUGH, I TRIED MY BEST TO REDUCE THE LIKELIHOOD OF THIS HAPPENING BUT ULTIMATELY IT'S UP TO YOU TO FIND OUT WHAT WORKS AND WHAT DOESN'T.
